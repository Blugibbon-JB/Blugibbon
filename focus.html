Your jobs inbox link

You open the jobs inbox as a delegate, so Gmail gives you your own address for it. Save it once and every Email link will open in the jobs inbox.

  1. In Gmail, open the jobs inbox (the delegated mailbox) in a new tab.
  2. Copy the whole address from the address bar. It looks like https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/d/…/#inbox.
  3. Paste it below and save.
That doesn't look like a Gmail address. It should start with https://mail.google.com/mail/

To change it later, hold Alt (Option on a Mac) and click any Email link.
